# TA/DA Rules for Faculty (synthetic, for coursework only)

## §1 Scope
These rules apply to official travel by faculty members of the Institute, including travel funded from research grants, conference travel and travel for institute work.

## §2 Travel sanction
Every official journey requires prior sanction on Form TA-1, approved by the Dean (Research) for grant-funded travel and by the Chair of the Area for other travel. In an emergency, travel may be undertaken without prior sanction, but post-facto approval must be obtained from the Dean within 7 days of return. Claims without sanction will not be processed.

## §3.1 Mode and class of travel
Faculty are entitled to air travel in economy class. Business class is not admissible under any head. Train travel is admissible in AC 2-tier; Professors may travel AC 1st class. Tickets should be booked directly with the airline or through the Institute travel desk. Convenience fees of third-party booking platforms are not reimbursed.

## §3.2 Hotel accommodation
<!-- topic: hotel_cap -->
Hotel charges are reimbursed on actuals up to a ceiling per night: ₹6,000 in X-class cities, ₹4,500 in Y-class cities and ₹3,000 in Z-class cities. Charges above the ceiling are borne by the faculty member.

## §3.3 Daily allowance
<!-- topic: da_rate -->
Daily allowance (DA) is ₹1,000 per day of absence from headquarters. DA covers meals and incidental expenses and needs no bills.

## §3.4 Local conveyance
Local conveyance (taxi, auto, metro) at the place of visit is reimbursed on actuals up to ₹800 per day, supported by receipts.

## §4 Conference registration
<!-- topic: registration_cap -->
Registration fees for domestic conferences may be reimbursed up to ₹30,000 per conference, on production of the receipt and proof of attendance.

## §5.1 Submission deadline
<!-- topic: deadline -->
TA/DA claims must be submitted within 60 days of return to headquarters.

## §5.2 Documents
Every claim must carry the sanctioned Form TA-1, tickets, hotel invoices, and the registration receipt where applicable.

## §6 Advances
A TA advance of up to 80% of the estimated admissible amount may be drawn on sanctioned travel. The advance must be settled within 30 days of return, failing which it is recovered from salary.
