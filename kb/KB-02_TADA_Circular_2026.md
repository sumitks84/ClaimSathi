# Circular ACC/2026/07: Revision of TA/DA rates (synthetic)

## §1 City classification
X-class cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad and Pune. Y-class cities: Patna, Lucknow, Jaipur, Bhubaneswar, Ranchi, Kochi, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Goa and Indore. All other places, including Gaya and Bodh Gaya, are Z-class.

## §2 Revised hotel ceiling
<!-- topic: hotel_cap -->
With effect from 1 April 2026, hotel charges are reimbursed on actuals up to ₹7,500 per night in X-class cities, ₹5,000 per night in Y-class cities and ₹3,500 per night in Z-class cities. The hotel invoice must show the Institute as guest organisation or the faculty member's name.

## §3 Revised daily allowance
<!-- topic: da_rate -->
Daily allowance is revised to ₹1,200 per day of absence from headquarters. Where the conference or host provides free meals, DA is admissible at 50%, that is ₹600 per day.

## §4 Conference-designated hotel exception
Where the conference organiser designates the venue hotel and its tariff exceeds the ceiling, the excess may be admitted on production of the organiser's letter, with endorsement of the Dean (Research). Without the endorsement, the ceiling applies.

## §5 Revised submission deadline
<!-- topic: deadline -->
TA/DA claims must be submitted within 30 days of return to headquarters. Claims received later require condonation of delay by the Director.
