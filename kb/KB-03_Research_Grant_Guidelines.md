# Faculty Seed Grant Guidelines 2025 (synthetic)

## §1 Purpose
Seed grants support early-stage research by faculty members. A seed grant of up to ₹3,00,000 is sanctioned for two years.

## §2 Budget heads
The sanctioned amount is split across four heads: Travel, Data and Software, Research Assistance, and Contingency.

## §3 Travel head limit
<!-- topic: travel_head -->
Expenditure under the Travel head may not exceed 40% of the sanctioned grant. Travel claims are charged to the Travel head only if sufficient balance remains in that head.

## §4 Re-appropriation
Funds may be moved between budget heads only with written approval of the Dean (Research), requested on Form RG-3 before the expenditure is incurred.

## §5 Conference registration
<!-- topic: registration_cap -->
Registration fees for domestic conferences may be charged to the seed grant up to ₹40,000 per conference.

## §6 International conferences
International conference travel requires prior approval of the Dean (Research) and is limited to one trip per grant year. The paper must be accepted for presentation.

## §7 Utilisation certificate
The Principal Investigator submits a utilisation certificate at the end of each grant year. Unspent balance lapses at the end of the grant period.
