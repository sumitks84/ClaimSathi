# SOP: Submission and processing of reimbursement claims (synthetic)

## §1 Submission
Claims are submitted on the claims portal with scanned bills, and original bills are deposited with the Accounts Section within 7 days of online submission.

## §2 Verification
The Accounts Section verifies each claim against the TA/DA rules, the sanction on Form TA-1 and the grant head balance. Claims with deficiencies are returned to the claimant with reasons.

## §3 Mandatory documents
Air travel claims need the e-ticket and boarding passes for every sector. Hotel claims need an itemised hotel invoice showing the hotel's GSTIN. Conference claims need the registration receipt and certificate of attendance or presentation.

## §4 Missing bills and self-certificate
A self-certificate in lieu of bills is accepted only for local conveyance, up to ₹500 per item and ₹2,000 in total per claim. Self-certificates are not accepted for air tickets, train tickets, hotel charges or registration fees.

## §5 Processing time
The service standard is 15 working days from receipt of complete documents. Status codes on the portal are Submitted, Under Verification, Returned, Recommended and Paid.

## §6 Grievances
Delays beyond the service standard can be raised with the Assistant Registrar (Accounts) through the helpdesk.
