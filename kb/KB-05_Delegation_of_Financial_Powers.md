# Delegation of financial powers: extract (synthetic, staff only)

## §1 Approval of reimbursement claims
The Accounts Officer may approve reimbursement claims up to ₹50,000. The FA&CAO may approve claims up to ₹2,00,000. Claims above ₹2,00,000 require approval of the Director.

## §2 Condonation and exceptions
Condonation of delay in submission of claims is a power of the Director. Exceptions to hotel ceilings for conference-designated hotels are endorsed by the Dean (Research) before approval by the competent authority.
